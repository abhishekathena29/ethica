Overview

Food choices made clearer

Food labels are long and easy to miss. Ethica helps you understand what you are buying or eating — whether it fits your diet, whether it may contain allergens you avoid, and how it scores for health, environment, and animal impact. Use it in the store aisle or at the table.

🛡️

Safer for you

Match products to vegan, vegetarian, Jain, gluten-free, and other preferences — plus the allergens you care about.

🌍

Clearer for the planet

See carbon, water, and animal-impact signals so you can choose with your values in mind.

✨

Better alternatives

When a product is not a good fit, Ethica suggests options that better match your needs.

How it works

Simple steps, clear answers

Set your preferences

Tell Ethica which diets and allergens matter to you. Adjust how strict “may contain” warnings should be.

Scan a product or plate

Use the barcode camera, snap a label photo, or run Plate Check. Ethica looks up product data and analyzes ingredients.

Review the result

Get a clear safety signal, scores, and details. Explore better alternatives if the product is not a good match.

Save your decision

Mark whether you bought, avoided, or chose something else — so your history and impact stay meaningful.

Who it’s for

User roles

Ethica is a personal consumer app. There is no separate admin console inside the app.

Signed-in user

Create an account with Google, Apple, or email. Your preferences and scan history can sync across devices. You can sign out or request account deletion.

Guest user

Try Ethica without an account. Scanning and local preferences work on your device. Cloud sync is limited until you sign in.

Privacy & security

Your trust matters

You control preferences

Diets and allergens stay under your control. Change or clear them anytime in Settings.

Encrypted connections

Account and cloud sync traffic use secure HTTPS connections.

Clear policies

FAQ

Common questions

Is Ethica free to try?

You can open the app and use core scanning features. Sign in if you want cloud sync for preferences and history.

Do I need an account?

No. Guest mode lets you use Ethica on your device. An account helps keep preferences and history available when you reinstall or switch devices.

How accurate are the results?

Ethica combines product database data with AI analysis. Labels and manufacturing practices can change. Always double-check packaging for critical allergen decisions.

What cameras and photos are used for?

Camera and photo access are used to scan barcodes, ingredient labels, and plate photos for analysis. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete from the app Settings, or submit a request on our Account Deletion page.
