Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026 · Last updated: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ethica (“we,” “us,” or “our”), developed by Arham Jain, handles information when you use the Ethica mobile application and related support website. If you have questions, contact us at whitehatarham@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account and authentication information

If you create or sign in to an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name (when provided by you or your sign-in provider)
  • Authentication provider details (for example Google, Apple, or email sign-in)
  • Account identifiers and sign-in timestamps

If you continue as a guest, we do not require an email address. Guest use stays mainly on your device.

Preferences and profile settings

  • Selected diets and custom diet labels
  • Selected allergens and custom allergen labels
  • “May contain” sensitivity and GMO avoidance settings
  • Priority weights for health, environment, and ethics

Scan and usage content

  • Product names, barcodes, brands, and analysis results from your scans
  • Purchase decisions you record (purchased, avoided, or chose an alternative)
  • Interactions with suggested alternatives
  • Optional product reviews, issue reports, and missing-product submissions
  • Photos you capture or select for barcode, label, or plate analysis

Device permissions and related data

  • Camera: used to scan barcodes, labels, and plate photos
  • Photo library: used when you choose an existing image to analyze
  • Location (when allowed): used to help show nearby store information related to products

We do not use push-notification permission for marketing at this time.

Technical and diagnostic information

We may process limited technical information needed to run the service securely (such as authentication tokens on your device and basic request logs on our cloud provider). Ethica does not currently operate a separate advertising analytics SDK.

2. Why we collect information

  • To analyze ingredients and provide safety, health, environment, and ethics insights
  • To personalize results using your diets and allergens
  • To sync preferences and history when you are signed in
  • To suggest better alternatives and improve product matching
  • To support account management, security, and customer support
  • To investigate reports of incorrect analysis or missing products

3. How we use information

We use your information only to provide and improve Ethica. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your product photos for advertising. We do not sell your scan history.

4. How information is stored and protected

  • On your device: preferences, scan history, and related caches may be stored locally so the app works quickly and offline where possible.
  • In the cloud (signed-in users): account profile, preferences, scan history backups, reviews, reports, alternative interactions, and optional product submissions may be stored with our cloud authentication and database provider.
  • In transit: connections to our services use HTTPS encryption.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. We work to protect your information with reasonable technical and organizational measures.

5. Third-party services

Ethica relies on trusted third parties to provide the service:

  • Authentication and cloud database provider: account sign-in, session management, and synced app data
  • Google Gemini AI: ingredient, label, and plate analysis
  • Open Food Facts: public product database lookups for barcodes and product details
  • Google / Apple: optional sign-in providers you choose
  • Map / place data providers: optional nearby store lookup when location is enabled

These providers process data according to their own privacy policies and only as needed to deliver the requested feature.

6. Authentication and account information

When you sign in with Google, Apple, or email, we store the account details needed to recognize you and sync your data. Password reset for email accounts is handled through our authentication provider. Guest sessions do not create a full cloud account.

7. Uploaded content, images, and profile data

Images you scan or upload are processed to identify products or ingredients. Analysis results may be cached to improve performance. If you submit a missing product, review, or issue report, that content may be stored so we can review and improve the service. Profile fields such as display name and email are used for account identification and support.

8. Notifications

Ethica does not currently require push-notification permission for core features. If notification features are added later, we will update this policy and request permission where required.

9. Analytics and diagnostic data

We may use limited diagnostic information to keep the app reliable (for example, understanding failed sign-ins or sync errors). We do not sell analytics data to advertisers.

10. Data retention

  • Local scan history remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app
  • Signed-in cloud records are retained while your account is active
  • After an account deletion request, we aim to delete or de-identify personal account data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for security

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access or get a copy of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and related personal data
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions (camera, photos, location)
  • Clear local scan history inside the app

To exercise these rights, email whitehatarham@gmail.com or use the Account Deletion page.

12. Children’s privacy

Ethica is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

13. Account deletion

You may delete your account from the app Settings or by submitting a request on our Account Deletion page. Deletion removes access to your account and deletes personal data we no longer need, subject to limited legal or security retention. Guest data on a device can be removed by clearing history or uninstalling the app.

14. International users

Your information may be processed in the countries where we or our service providers operate. By using Ethica, you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the effective date above when we do. Continued use of Ethica after changes means you accept the updated policy.
