Delete your Ethica account

Account deletion may be permanent. Please read carefully before you continue.

Warning: deletion may be permanent

Once your account is deleted, you may lose access to synced preferences, cloud scan history, reviews, and other account data. This action often cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

Option 1 — In the Ethica app

  1. Open Ethica and go to Settings
  2. Sign in if you are not already signed in
  3. Choose Delete Account and confirm
  4. Local history and session data on that device are cleared

Option 2 — Request by form or email

If you cannot use the in-app option, submit the form below or email whitehatarham@gmail.com with the subject line “Ethica Account Deletion Request.”

What will be deleted

  • Your account sign-in access
  • Profile details linked to your account (such as email and display name)
  • Synced preferences and cloud-backed scan history associated with your account
  • Cloud records of reviews, issue reports, alternative interactions, and product submissions tied to your user ID, where technically feasible
  • Local app session and local history on devices where you complete in-app deletion

What may be retained

  • Records we must keep for legal, fraud-prevention, security, or accounting reasons
  • Anonymized or aggregated information that no longer identifies you
  • Backups that may take additional time to fully purge
  • Communications you sent to support, for a limited period needed to complete your request

Processing timeline

We aim to process account deletion requests within 7 business days of verification, and to complete deletion or de-identification of personal account data within 30 days. Complex cases may take longer; we will follow up by email if needed.

Guest users

If you used Ethica as a guest without creating an account, there may be no cloud account to delete. You can clear scan history in Settings or uninstall the app to remove local data from your device.
